{% comment %} A compound card, forms.CompoundCard (as card): its material, its runs, then its blocks of values (_value_block.html). It posts to views.add_compound, which shows what Check read. A reference's Add data page and the add-a-compound page both include it, with the editor's dialog (_draw_dialog.html) and the scripts for its Draw button (draw.js), its phase steps (phase_steps.js) and its checks as the curator types (checks.js). What the server makes of the card goes in two boxes, one after the material and one after the runs. Check fills them when the page comes back. checks.js fills them as the curator types: a part marked data-check names its box, and the box's data-check-url is the address that reads that part. Each box is a live region (aria-live), so a screen reader reads each new answer. {% endcomment %}
